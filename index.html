Immediate actions · no tests · no new learning phases · Sep 16

Sell today.

Every action below can be executed in Ads Manager in under a minute and none of them resets the one ad set that carries all the signal. The math to hold in mind: 2,341 landing-page views produced 2 purchases, one sale per 1,170 views.

1

Pause ecomm Adset 4 and ecomm Adset 5 now.

Both were created Sep 15 to 16. They are brand-new learning phases with the same states, same age range and the same ads as Adset 2. Adset 4 spent $51.18 on Sep 15 for 130 impressions and 3 landing-page views, a CPM of $393.69 against $23.87 on Adset 2 the same day. They split budget and auction pressure away from the ad set that already has 1,710 landing-page views and both purchases.

Frees $300 per day with zero learning cost
2

Raise ecomm Adset 2 from $360 to $500 today, $600 tomorrow.

Adset 2 already delivered $445.64 on Sep 15 for 370 landing-page views at $1.20 each, so the algorithm is already pacing near $450. It is at 2 conversions in 7 days against the 50 needed to exit learning: there is no exited-learning state to lose, so a budget increase cannot set it back. Two steps instead of one keeps the daily change under 40% and keeps cost per LPV from spiking.

Same ad set, same signal, ~40% more views today
3

Pause the four Courtney ads inside Adset 2.

Last 7 days inside Adset 2: Courtney ads spent $91.54 for 7 landing-page views ($13.08 per view) and zero purchases. Julie 65-second video: $1,011.19 for 1,322 views ($0.76 per view) and 1 purchase. Julie static: $564.04 for 50 views and 1 purchase. Pausing ads inside an ad set does not restart the ad set. Keep both Julie ads live.

100% of spend on the two ads that have ever sold
4

Do not touch Adset 2 targeting today. Read the age data anyway.

Across all 13 days, 65+ took 49% of landing-page views (1,144) and 55 to 64 took 27% (628), with zero purchases between them. Both purchases came from 45 to 54, which had only 13% of views (303). Two sales are not decision-grade and 65+ is also the cheapest traffic at $1.69 per view. Editing age on Adset 2 is a targeting change and would restart it, so it stays as is. The daily age slides below make the shift visible day by day.

No reset. The signal is logged, not acted on blind
5

The sales you need today come from the checkout, not the auction.

At the observed one sale per 1,170 landing-page views, even $600 a day on Adset 2 delivers roughly 450 views and about 0.4 sales per day. Ten sales in seven days needs the landing page to convert at least 3x better or warm traffic that costs nothing to buy: the ActiveCampaign list and every lead on file should receive the $179 per month offer today, and the $179 per month price must be the first thing visible above the fold on the landing page and the checkout.

Zero media cost, zero learning, same day
