Where the 1.6× lift comes from

Three levers.
All active by
Tuesday night.

Traffic is not the problem. Cost per landing-page view fell 71 percent from Sep 11 at $3.86 to Sep 14 at $0.71. The bottleneck is the last step, and it is a single-week fix.

Lever 01

Checkout page

+80%

Anchor the $179 by 5 and $799 upfront paths above the fold. Add trust row: guarantee, secure badge, refund window. Remove any field not required for Stripe. Persist selection across retries. Verify Meta fbc passes on all three checkout copies before Wednesday.

Lever 02

Creative rotation

+40%

Adset 1 is inactive at $190. Reactivate only if the new creative lands. Push spend into the Sep 14 winner: CTR 3.04 percent, cost per LPV $0.71. Launch two new hooks Tuesday: Julie masterclass, biological-age result. Compare 1-day and 7-day click windows.

Lever 03

Retargeting layer

+2 sales

1,268 landing-page views in six days is a real remarketing pool. Warm audience of LPV in the last 14 days, $50 a day retargeting ad set starting Tuesday. Message is risk reversal: refund window, payment plan, secure checkout. Historically delivers 1.2× to 2× baseline CVR.
